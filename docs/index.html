A quiet home for your menu bar.

One small icon. One stacked list. Instant search. Iguana tucks away the clutter and gets out of your way.

Open interactive mockupSee what's inside

Design mockup. Browse the stack, or just type.

Made for focus

Stacked, not strung out

Every item lives in one vertical list: pinned first, then the stack, then hidden. No second bar, no overlay, no notch games.

Noise-free search

Open with ⌃⌥␣, type two letters, press ↩. Matches are highlighted; everything else disappears.

Pin what matters

Press P on any row to keep it in view. Rarely used items sink quietly into Hidden.

Keyboard first

Arrow keys to move, Return to open, Esc to leave. The mouse works too, of course.

Looks native

Translucent material, system fonts, automatic light and dark. It feels like part of macOS.

Private by design

No account, no network, no analytics. Your menu bar stays yours.

Targets for the first build. Native Swift, no web view, no timers while the panel is closed.

How it works

  1. Click the iguana (or press the shortcut). The panel opens under the menu bar.
  2. Browse or type. The list narrows as you type; the best match is already selected.
  3. Press Return. The item opens exactly as if you had clicked it in the bar.
